Our Story

Engineering Sri Lanka's Infrastructure Since 1999

TRITECH Marketing International (Pvt) Ltd. is one of Sri Lanka's most trusted engineering solution providers, built on over two and a half decades of uncompromising precision.

We serve as the exclusive authorized dealer for world-renowned brands including Speroni (Italy), Calpeda (Italy), TSURUMI (Japan), EVERGUSH, Heng Long, Aquatherm, and Puhui — delivering complete civil, mechanical, plumbing and environmental engineering solutions across the nation.

From designing custom pump packages to installing fully integrated systems with premium motors and intelligent control panels, we deliver end-to-end excellence on every project — large or small.

Our Direction Vision & Mission
